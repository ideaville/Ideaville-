# Storyboard

Four frames. The camera never cuts. Same 1920×1080 stage the whole time.

## 1. Atmosphere — 1.0s

A late-October dusk over a stock-brick terrace. Sky is ink at the top and brick-dust at the eaves, not purple and not a sunset disc. Three houses, one roof, chimneys breaking the ridge. The middle house has a single sash already lit, quietly. A pollarded plane is cropped at the left edge. A lantern stands at the right. The road is empty of type except the wordmark and the place slate. It should feel like arriving on the street, not like a title sequence warming up.

## 2. Claim — 3.4s

“Know the house.” is in the lower left, large Fraunces, cream on the road. Nothing else has entered the lower third. The sentence is the whole point of this beat. The lit window has not flared yet — the house is still just a house.

## 3. Proof — 7.2s

The yellow band arrow has landed on the right, level with the headline. It is one D, the current-band color from the certificate, not a column of A–G. Under the headline: “Live EPC lookup. Retrofit need. Scheme fit.” The hero window is brighter than at frame 2, as if the lookup turned the light on. This frame has to be readable in one glance: claim, band, what the tool does.

## 4. Lockup — 9.7s

“Check a postcode” sits under the arrow, rule fully drawn. Everything else is still. This is the poster. If a frame is used alone, it is this one.

## Do not add

- A second scene, a zoom, or a cut
- Cards, panels, charts, maps, or a fake product UI
- A question mark, a loading state, or typed characters
- House numbers or a named resident
- Extra lines of copy, a logo bug animation, or music-visualiser motion
