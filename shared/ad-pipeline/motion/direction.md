# Motion direction — Ideaville 10s

Locked to `brief.md`. One composition. No dashboard, no purple, no extra headlines.

## What the viewer should remember

A Greenwich terrace at dusk, the word Ideaville, the line “Know the house.”, a single EPC band D, and the action “Check a postcode.”

## Audience

Practitioners doing energy and EPC work for Greenwich and SE London homes — not a consumer fintech audience. The proof is specific: postcode `SE18 3EU` (the register sample the tool was verified on). No door number, no resident name.

## Palette

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night ink | `#12181a` | Sky top, road, type field |
| Dusk teal | `#2c3a3c` | Mid sky |
| Brick dust | `#c9a27c` | Horizon only, desaturated |
| Stock brick | `#7d493c` / `#915744` / `#6e4036` | Three houses, hero warmer |
| Stucco | `#efe6d4` → `#d9cdb6` | Ground floor |
| Slate | `#3e464a` | One continuous roof |
| Cream | `#f6f1e7` | All type and the CTA rule |
| Band D | `#ffd500` | The only saturated signal |

Window light is the same warm family as band D, because the light is the house, not a second brand color. No amber UI accent competing with the certificate yellow.

## Type

- Brand: Outfit 600, tracked small caps `IDEAVILLE`. Company line under it in Outfit 400: Alpha Performance Solutions.
- Headline: Fraunces roman, optical size max, weight 560. “Know the house.”
- Support: Outfit 400. “Live EPC lookup. Retrofit need. Scheme fit.”
- Slate: Outfit tracked “Greenwich · SE London” plus IBM Plex Mono `SE18 3EU`.
- CTA: Outfit 500 “Check a postcode” with a cream rule that draws once.

## Composition

1920×1080. The terrace is the picture. Type sits in a lower-third road, not in cards.

- Top left: wordmark and company. Static from frame 0.
- Top right: place slate and postcode. Static from frame 0.
- Picture: one terrace row, shared roof, three chimneys, one lit sash, a cropped plane tree, a street lamp, a hazy spire in the right gap.
- Lower left: headline, then support.
- Lower right: EPC D arrow, then the CTA.

The arrow is the certificate, drawn as one right-pointing band label. It is not a chart, a chip, or a badge stuck on the brick.

## Three motions

1. **Drift** — distant spire moves 32px left and the terrace 12px left across the whole 10s. Pavement seam, road, and type stay put.
2. **Resolve** — at 2.0s the headline settles up into place. At 5.5s the band D arrow stamps in and the hero window brightens; the support line fades with that stamp. One idea, two beats: the claim, then the proof.
3. **Lock** — at 8.5s the CTA arrives and its rule draws. Hold to 10.0.

No other entrances. Brand and place are already there (hero-first). No typing, no counters, no parallax on the type.
